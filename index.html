03 / Quick tips

Stay safe online

A few small habits can make a big difference.

🔒

Use strong passwords

Use unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication where possible.

💬

Think before sharing

Never post your address, phone number or private details publicly.

🔗

Be careful with links

Avoid unknown links and check the sender before opening attachments.

⚙️

Check privacy settings

Review who can see your posts, profile and personal information.

📰

Spot fake news

Check the source and compare information before sharing it.

💗

Be kind online

Respect others, report abuse and never join cyberbullying.

Pause. Check. Then click.

07 / Spot the warning signs

Spam & phishing awareness

Scammers often try to create urgency or trick you into sharing sensitive information.

🎣

Suspicious links

Check the website address carefully. Don't open unexpected links or attachments, even if a message looks familiar.

⏰

Urgent threats

Messages claiming your account will close immediately may be trying to rush you. Verify through the official app or website.

🔑

Requests for secrets

Never share passwords, PINs, OTPs or banking details with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

Received a suspicious message?

Don't reply, click or forward it. Verify independently and report it through the appropriate official channel.

08 / Get help

Emergency cyber help in India

If you experience cyber financial fraud, act quickly and contact official support.

📞

Call 1930

For cyber financial fraud in India, call the national helpline 1930 as soon as possible.

🖥️

Report online

Use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to submit a complaint.

Open official portal ↗
🏦

Contact your bank

If money or banking details are involved, contact your bank using the number on its official website or card.

Keep transaction IDs, messages and screenshots as evidence. Never share an OTP or PIN with anyone claiming to help.
